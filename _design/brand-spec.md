No `WebSearch` fact-check was required under Core Principle #0: the brief names no real
product, version or specification. Halcyon and every figure in the mocks are invented.

## Brand attributes (shared across all three directions)

- **Institutional, not startup.** This is a tool a bank's risk function would sign off on.
- **Quiet.** One accent colour, used to mean one thing. No second accent for decoration.
- **Literal.** Labels say what things are. No cute naming.
- **Tabular.** Figures are monospaced and right-aligned. Always. This is the brand's
  single strongest signature and it is inherited from banking's own printed conventions.

## Forbidden (this project's red lines)

- The GitHub-dark lazy solution: flat `#0D1117` field + generic cyan/violet neon glow.
  Dark is mandated; *that particular* dark is banned.
- Purple-to-blue "AI gradient" of any kind.
- Emoji as iconography.
- Rounded card + coloured left border accent.
- Green "success" / red "danger" as the only semantic colours — too dashboard-generic.
- Any chart treatment Recharts cannot reproduce.

## Accessibility floor (non-negotiable in every direction)

- Body text ≥ 14px, labels/captions ≥ 12px.
- Body contrast ≥ 4.5:1 against its own background. Measured, not eyeballed.
- Charts never encode meaning by colour alone — always paired with a label or pattern.
- Visible focus ring on every interactive element.
